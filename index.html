Cargo-packing roguelite

UNDECLARED

You bought the ship on credit. Now every trip has to pay for it, and greed is the whole game.

  • Buy crates in port and pack them into the hold.
  • Each crate changes its neighbour's price. Fuel grows with weight.
  • Customs searches from the top down. Hide what they shouldn't find.
  • Roguelite
  • Puzzle
  • Coming to Steam

Folk-horror tactics roguelike

Black Rye

Harvest time, and something walks out of the rye. You go after it with grandpa's pitchfork.

  • Start as a peasant. Become a knight, hunter or mage by what you find and wear.
  • Up to four companions join on the road, and you gear them too.
  • A d20 decides who moves first. After that, no misses: every hit is shown before you make it.
  • Tactics
  • Roguelike
  • Coming to Steam
